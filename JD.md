              About the job     


Company Description: Talknlock is a digital marketing leader specializing in product management for websites and mobile applications, with a strong focus on intuitive, user-centered experiences. The company designs engaging, visually appealing interfaces and develops data-driven digital strategies that enhance brand presence, reach, and conversion. Using agile and scrum methodologies, Talknlock manages the full product lifecycle from concept to delivery, ensuring solutions align with market demands and client goals. Innovation, agility, and customer-centricity guide the organization’s approach, supported by continuous insights from real-time user feedback and market trends. Talknlock actively shares updates, thought leadership, and digital marketing trends, inviting collaboration and engagement through online channels.   


Role Description: As an Artificial Intelligence Intern at Talknlock, you will support the design and development of AI-driven solutions that enhance digital marketing and product experiences. You will assist in collecting, preprocessing, and analyzing data, experimenting with machine learning models, and evaluating their performance in real-world scenarios. Daily tasks include collaborating with product and engineering teams, writing and testing code, documenting experiments, and preparing concise reports on findings and recommendations. You will also help integrate AI features into web and mobile products, contributing to prototypes and proof-of-concept initiatives. This is a full-time, on-site role based in Jaipur.

Qualifications         
Strong foundation in Computer Science, including core concepts such as algorithms, data structures, and software development principles.
Demonstrated Analytical Skills and exposure to Data Science methods for data cleaning, exploration, and basic statistical analysis.
Knowledge of Machine Learning concepts, model training and evaluation, and familiarity with common ML libraries or frameworks.
Programming skills in languages commonly used in AI and data science (such as Python), including experience with version control and debugging.
Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or a related field.
Ability to work on-site in Jaipur, collaborate in cross-functional teams, and communicate technical ideas clearly in written and spoken form.
Curiosity about AI applications in digital marketing and product management; prior internship, academic projects, or personal portfolios in AI or data science are a plus.
Familiarity with agile practices or scrum environments and an openness to learning new tools and technologies quickly.
